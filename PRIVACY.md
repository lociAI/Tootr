# Privacy Policy for Tootr Pro DJ

**Effective Date: June 13, 2026**

Tootr Pro DJ ("the App") is developed by Jtech Software. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

## 1. Data Collection
The App **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information**. We do not have access to your contacts, location, or any other private data.

## 2. Audio Recordings
The App includes a feature that allows users to record audio. All recordings are stored **locally on your device**. These files are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third parties by the App. You have full control over these files and can delete them at any time.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Because we do not collect any personal data, the App is safe for users of all ages.
